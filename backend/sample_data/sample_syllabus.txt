Engineering Mathematics - B.Tech Syllabus
============================================

Unit 1: Linear Algebra and Matrices
-----------------------------------
Chapter: Matrices and Determinants
Topics:
- Matrix operations and types
- Rank of a matrix
- Eigenvalues and eigenvectors
- Cayley-Hamilton theorem
- Diagonalization of matrices

Chapter: Systems of Linear Equations
Topics:
- Solution of linear systems
- Gaussian elimination method
- Gauss-Jordan elimination
- Consistency and rank method

Unit 2: Calculus
-----------------------------------
Chapter: Differential Calculus
Topics:
- Limits and continuity
- Derivatives and differentiation rules
- Mean value theorem
- Taylor's and Maclaurin's series
- L'Hopital's rule

Chapter: Integral Calculus
Topics:
- Indefinite integrals
- Methods of integration
- Definite integrals
- Application to areas and volumes

Unit 3: Differential Equations
-----------------------------------
Chapter: Ordinary Differential Equations
Topics:
- First-order differential equations
- Separable equations
- Exact equations
- Linear differential equations
- Second-order differential equations with constant coefficients

Chapter: Partial Differential Equations
Topics:
- Formation of partial differential equations
- Solution of first-order linear PDEs
- Wave equation and heat equation

Unit 4: Complex Analysis
-----------------------------------
Chapter: Complex Numbers and Functions
Topics:
- Complex numbers and operations
- Modulus and argument
- De Moivre's theorem
- Functions of complex variables

Chapter: Analytic Functions
Topics:
- Cauchy-Riemann equations
- Harmonic functions
- Singularities and poles
- Residue theorem

Unit 5: Vector Calculus
-----------------------------------
Chapter: Vector Analysis
Topics:
- Scalar and vector fields
- Gradient, divergence, and curl
- Line integrals
- Surface integrals
- Stokes' theorem and divergence theorem

Unit 6: Probability and Statistics
-----------------------------------
Chapter: Probability
Topics:
- Sample space and events
- Conditional probability
- Bayes' theorem
- Probability distributions

Chapter: Statistics
Topics:
- Mean, median, and mode
- Variance and standard deviation
- Normal distribution
- Hypothesis testing
