Engineering Mathematics - Question Paper 2023
================================================

Exam Date: 15-06-2023

Section A (2 marks each)

1. Define the rank of a matrix and explain its significance. [2]

2. State the Cayley-Hamilton theorem. (2 marks)

3. What are eigenvalues and eigenvectors? (2)

4. Find the derivative of x^3 + 2x^2 - 5 with respect to x. [2]

5. Define continuity of a function at a point. (2 marks)

Section B (5 marks each)

6. Find the inverse of the matrix [[1, 2], [3, 4]] using the adjugate method. [5]

7. Solve the system of equations:
   2x + 3y + z = 9
   x + y + z = 5
   3x - y + 2z = 8
   (5 marks)

8. Using Gaussian elimination, find the rank of the matrix [[1, 2, 3], [4, 5, 6], [7, 8, 9]]. [5]

9. Find the integral of cos(x) * e^x using integration by parts. (5 marks)

10. Evaluate the definite integral from 0 to 1 of (2x + 3x^2) dx. [5]

Section C (10 marks each)

11. Find all eigenvalues and eigenvectors of the matrix [[4, 1], [2, 3]]. (10 marks)

12. Solve the differential equation dy/dx + 2y = e^(-2x) using integrating factor method. [10]

13. Verify Stokes' theorem for the vector field F = (y, z, x) over the unit sphere. (10 marks)

14. Find the Taylor series expansion of sin(x) about x = 0 up to the 5th degree term. [10]

15. A box contains 5 red balls and 3 black balls. If 2 balls are drawn without replacement, find the probability that both are red. (10 marks)
